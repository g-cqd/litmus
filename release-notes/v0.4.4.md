## Behaviour

- Rejects XML `<!DOCTYPE>` / `<!ENTITY>` declarations and NUL bytes and disables external entity resolution in `XcodeTestConfiguration.swift`.
- Uses constant-time HMAC-SHA256 verification on `LITMUS_STORE_KEY` result records in `ResultStore.swift`.
- Verifies cached simulator helper binaries and resolved `simctl` binaries through `BinaryTrustChecker` in `SimulatorHarness.swift`.
- Rebuilt on `analyzerkit` 0.5.6 and the repinned Aemi packages.
