## Behaviour

- `remove-view-action` also empties `Button` actions (the `action:` closure, or the trailing closure after an unlabeled title or before a `label:` closure; never a label) and `.onTapGesture` / `.onLongPressGesture` closures.
- `neutralize-view-modifier`: `.opacity(x)` to `0`, `.allowsHitTesting(x)` to `!(x)`. The modifier stays, so the view's static type does not change.
- `remove-accessibility-metadata`: a non-empty literal `.accessibilityLabel` / `.accessibilityHint` to `""`, `.accessibilityHidden(x)` to `!(x)`.
