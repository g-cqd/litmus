## Corrections to preliminary investigation

- The 2098 unmatched name-reference sites are neither an upper nor lower sound bound on uncovered mutants.
- analyzerkit's conditional compilation model does not import a complete Xcode build configuration; `canImport` is not modeled.
- swift-mutation-testing 1.6.4 already has IfConfig handling, caches, test hashes and schema batches (16 mutants/function).
- Its implicit getter limitation is partly a scope-discovery implementation issue, not a Swift prohibition on all getters.
- The 6.5-minute observation was product-timestamp evidence during a build/test attempt, not a completed benchmark.
- A small successful expression probe is not a semantic proof for arbitrary effects, ownership, overloads or builders.
- A failed helper probe rejects that attempted helper, not every possible helper design.
- Deadwood/constant conditions/clones do not justify equivalent-mutant pruning or shared verdicts.
- A family report reader needs real CLI/artifact/provenance/Danger changes, not only one new invocation line.
